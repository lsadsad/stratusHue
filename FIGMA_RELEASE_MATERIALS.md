## 📄 Description (300-500 words)

```
Stratus Hue is a powerful organization tool that helps designers manage complex Figma files with visual color coding, intelligent bookmarking, and seamless navigation.

🎨 SMART COLOR CODING
Add color emojis to layers and pages for instant visual organization. The plugin automatically switches between square emojis for layers and circle emojis for pages, making it easy to establish your own color-coding system. Whether you're marking approval status, priority levels, or design versions, Stratus Hue adapts to your workflow.

📍 INTELLIGENT BOOKMARKING
Save important design elements as "anchors" with one click. Each bookmark remembers its page location and lets you jump back instantly—perfect for large files with hundreds of layers. The resync feature keeps your bookmarks up-to-date when you rename layers or reorganize pages.

🔄 BROWSER-LIKE NAVIGATION
Navigate through your selection history with familiar Back and Forward buttons, just like a web browser. The plugin automatically tracks your selections across pages, making it effortless to switch between different areas of your design without losing your place.

📅 SMART DATE TAGGING
Add today's date to layers or page titles with one click. Stratus Hue automatically replaces old dates, ensuring your timestamps stay current. Perfect for versioning, tracking iterations, or marking review stages.

✨ CLEAN, INTUITIVE INTERFACE
Built with a modern dark theme and thoughtful UX, Stratus Hue feels natural to use. Collapsible sections keep your workspace clean, while smart truncation handles long names elegantly. The plugin remembers your preferences and adapts to your workflow.

WHO IS THIS FOR?
• Product designers managing complex design systems
• UI/UX teams collaborating on large projects
• Design leads organizing files for handoff
• Anyone working with multi-page Figma documents

Stratus Hue transforms chaotic layer panels into organized, navigable workspaces. Stop scrolling endlessly through layers—start navigating with purpose.
```
