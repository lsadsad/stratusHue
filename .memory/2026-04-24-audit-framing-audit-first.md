The template methodology audit (aud) is an independent effort — the findings drive tooling decisions, not the other way around. stratusHue is one possible downstream application but may not be the solution. This framing was explicitly corrected during Sahiti onboarding (2026-04-23).

**Why:** Early docs framed the audit as 'before building stratusHue' — that was wrong and risks anchoring decisions prematurely.

**How to apply:** Never frame the audit as being in service of stratusHue. The audit produces a finalized template spec; what that feeds into is decided after Phase 6.
