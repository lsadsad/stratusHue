    Controls

    Control layer hierarchy, element movement, zoom, and actions. Use arrow keys to move between buttons, Enter or Space to activate.
    Movement & Zoom
    Movement and zoom buttons arranged in 3 rows and 3 columns. Move selected elements by 1 pixel, or 8 with Shift. Resize with Ctrl. Duplicate with Alt. Use arrow keys to navigate, Enter or Space to activate buttons.
    PAGE Hierarchy
    Grid of hierarchy buttons arranged in 3 rows and 3 columns. Use arrow keys to navigate, Enter or Space to activate buttons.
    Sizing Modes
    Cycle through auto layout sizing modes. Use Tab to navigate, Enter or Space to activate buttons.

    SETTINGS

    🎨 Appearance

    Use arrow keys to navigate between theme options. Press Enter or Space to select a theme.
